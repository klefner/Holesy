## QA Review: Backlog Addition For AI Intelligence And Competitiveness

### Scope Reviewed
- Product backlog update for rival AI intelligence / efficiency scaling
- Consistency with the existing difficulty toggle entry
- Alignment with the stated design goal that the player should be able to lose on points

### Findings
- No critical issues found
- The new item correctly extends the existing difficulty toggle instead of creating a disconnected duplicate system
- The backlog now explicitly requires:
  - variablized rival intelligence / efficiency
  - at least one materially smarter rival in every game
  - a balance target where the player can lose on points

### Design Guidance
- “Smarter” should be treated as route-value efficiency under growth constraints, not just raw movement speed or unfair stat inflation
- This item should remain backlog/governance only until it is sequenced into implementation work

### QA Outcome
- Approved
