# QA Review - Master 16.142 Prism Orbit Cosmetic

## Scope

- Deliver one visible, gameplay-earned cosmetic for player judgment.
- Make Run Goal reward language truthful and specific.

## Behavior

- The first completed Run Goal unlocks `Prism Orbit`.
- Seven differently colored motes orbit and pulse inside the player hole.
- The unlock is stored in `holesy.objectMastery.v1` and automatically restored/equipped on later visits.
- Goal copy says either `Prism Orbit unlock` or `Prism Orbit equipped`; generic `cosmetic progress` language is removed.
- Later completed goals award mastery without falsely claiming another cosmetic.

## Remaining Product Work

- Cosmetic inventory and equip/unequip controls.
- Additional cosmetics and achievement mappings.
- Titles, skins, sound packs, and commercial cosmetic catalog architecture.
