# QA Review - Master 16.153 Animated Park Parcels

## Player-Entertainment Goal

Increase environmental discovery, consumable-object variety, and readable activity by turning whole parcels into visibly different animated public spaces.

## Park Catalogue

1. Playground: swings, slide, jungle gym, and children.
2. Basketball park: court, hoops, teams, and bouncing basketball.
3. Baseball park: diamond, bases, stands, stadium lights, players, ball, and cheering crowd.
4. Tennis park: court, sectional net, players, and moving tennis ball.
5. Running park: oval track and moving runners.
6. Swimming park: sectional pool water, swimmers, diving boards, and maintenance building.
7. Picnic park: tables, grills, cooks, fire-area activity, and animated smoke.
8. Fountain garden: basin, statue, animated water, formal layout, and valet parking.
9. Dog park: dogs, visitors, fencing, and agility ramp.
10. Skate park: multiple ramps and moving skaters.

## Variation

- Normal parks use maintained public-park materials.
- Rundown parks use faded ground, damaged fence gaps, brown landscaping, and scattered litter.
- Fancy parks use brighter landscaping, formal planters, premium finishes, fountains, and valet elements.
- Four to six parcels are reserved before ordinary building population each wave.
- Archetypes are shuffled without repetition until the available park set is exhausted.

## Validation Focus

- Confirm no ordinary building occupies a selected park parcel.
- Verify all ten archetypes can appear over several city rebuilds.
- Verify each archetype can appear in all three conditions.
- Consume courts, turf sections, equipment, fences, people, balls, stands, lights, water sections, and maintenance structures independently.
- Verify park animation stops cleanly when its object is consumed and resets between waves.
- Check mobile frame pacing in a six-park city.
