The city calls it a game because that sounds safer than an incident report. Learn the rules, then eat the evidence.

Game Summary

The Hunger

You are a Hunger under downtown Aldine. Move, swallow, grow.

Anything smaller than your mouth can be recorded. Anything bigger can record you.

Controls

Desktop: steer with the mouse, WASD, or arrow keys.

Mobile: drag toward what you want to consume. Release to stop drifting.

Eating and Growing

Eat people, cars, trees, buildings, debris, aid, and smaller rival holes.

Score and size usually rise together. Your tier rises as the city forgets more of itself.

Bigger rivals can eat you. Smaller rivals can be swallowed, but Endless records them elsewhere and sends them back smaller.

Hazards and Aid

Soldiers arrive as containment escalates. Their fire can shrink you, and enough damage can kill you.

Parallax aid drops are bright, useful, and suspicious. Blue means speed, gold means instant mass, and green means bullet protection.

Cars can panic, skid, crash, and burn. Skyscrapers only fall when enough of them is inside the Hunger.

Game Modes

Timed: two minutes. Highest score wins when the records close.

When Timed ends, you may lock the score or risk continuing into Last Man Standing.

Last Man Standing: no timer. Score is noise. Survive until one Hunger remains.

Waves: four districts. Each wave is meaner. Score carries forward.

Endless Waves: the lockdown never closes. Every district returns harder. Every fifth wave resets the holes as the map is edited.

Difficulty

Normal is baseline pressure. Hard makes containment faster, scarcer, and sharper.

Ultra starts closer to full lockdown: earlier troops, less easy food, rarer towers, and less forgiving rivals.

Higher difficulty can improve rare recovery odds, but it also gives the city better teeth.

Winning

Timed and Waves: finish first on score and stay uneaten.

Last Man Standing: be the last Hunger alive.

Endless Waves: there is no final win. Survive, save, withdraw from Pause, or get consumed.

Archives

Click Archive to read recovered documents and starter field patterns.

Win, or safely withdraw from Endless while leading, for a rare chance to recover one document.

If you lose, the file is gone. No document drops on losses.

Some documents hint at secret buffs and achievements. Read the strange details, then try them in the city.

Field Patterns

Buffs are short powers. Eat the right things in the right pattern to wake one up.

Active buff cards show the name, effect, and timer.

Starter pattern: swallow the cyan Parallax beacon for x2 speed.

Starter pattern: swallow the gold Parallax cache for instant growth.

Starter pattern: eat 10 people within 15 seconds to pull nearby objects toward you.

Green Parallax aid gives temporary bullet protection when you find it.

There are more buffs and achievements hidden in the Archive. The documents give clues, not exact answers.

When a timed buff ends, it has a short cooldown before it can happen again.

Save and Load

Endless Waves can be saved from Pause.

Load Endless appears on the menu when a saved breach exists. It restores the wave, holes, timers, buffs, and board state.

Stats

Stats opens your ledger: wins, losses, best scores, best waves, recent runs, and end reasons.

The Archive remembers the story. Stats remembers what you did.

Click the build label in the corner to read the latest recovered patch notes.

Survival Notes

Do not chew skyscrapers until they fit. Warning text means the city is not ready to fall.

Aid can save you. Cars panic, skid, and crash because the street knows.
